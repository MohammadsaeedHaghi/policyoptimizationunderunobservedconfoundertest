Semi-synthetic dataset options from UCI

Survey + smoke tests for the paper's real-data section. Every number below is measured on the actual downloaded data (script: smoke_tests.py). Selection principle: a dataset earns a slot only if it either (i) sits in the O-W operating regime by the measured coupling diagnostic, or (ii) deliberately probes the regime boundary the alpha sweep predicts.

1. What makes the real-data section unrejectable

The alpha sweep gives a falsifiable prediction: O-W beats naive when the observed covariates proxy the hidden confounder well (corr ≳ 0.75), ties around 0.55–0.75, and loses below. So the strategy is NOT "find a dataset where we win" — it is to populate the coupling axis with real datasets and show the prediction holds at every point. A reviewer can reject a cherry-picked win; a validated diagnostic that says where the method should and should not be used is much harder to dismiss.

2. Three construction recipes

A — real covariates, synthetic confounding (ACIC-style). Bootstrap real X for realistic geometry; synthesize S with designed coupling, T with designed selection (matched-Γ protocol intact), and Y(0)/Y(1) for ground truth. Any tabular dataset works; the dataset contributes covariate realism. Already built once (Diabetes-A).
B — real X, S, T; synthetic outcomes only. Keep a REAL treatment decision and a REAL hidden variable; synthesize only Y(0)/Y(1) (needed for ground truth, calibrated to the real outcome). The coupling and Lambda are facts about the world, not knobs — strongest realism claim, but the regime is whatever the data gives. Already built once (Diabetes-B, out-of-regime by design).
C — classification-to-policy with a hidden informative feature. Standard bandit-literature conversion (Dudik et al.; POEM): actions = decisions, reward = correctness; confounding is created by HIDING an informative feature and letting the logging policy use it. The X–S coupling is real (how well the remaining features predict the hidden one); the selection strength is a knob, so the matched-Γ protocol applies exactly.

4. The candidates, ranked

1. Mushroom (UCI 73) — the near-in-regime showcase

n = 8,124  |  corr(X,S) = 0.71 (AUC 0.90)  |  realized Λ = — (design knob)  |  P(T) = —  |  S→Y shift = +0.97

Hidden confounder: odor not in {none,almond,anise} (hidden). Story: a forager decides whether to eat a mushroom by SMELL (odor is nearly deterministic for edibility: S→Y shift +0.97); the dataset we learn from records everything EXCEPT odor. Eating outcomes look great for the forager's picks — vitality-style selection, perfectly natural.

Proposed design: Recipe C. X = 20 morphological features, S = bad odor (hidden), logging policy eats with P(eat|S) set to hit Λ=5 (matched-Γ protocol identical to owgap). Y(eat) with asymmetric penalty for poisonous, Y(pass)=0; capped variant = 'eat at most 30%' basket budget. Measured corr(X,S)=0.71 (AUC 0.90) — just below the 0.75 in-regime line: quote as the near-boundary point, and (honestly disclosed) an X-augmented variant (+spore-print) can nudge coupling up.

Verdict: STRONG ADD. Real coupling near the regime boundary, huge S→Y, natural story, n=8,124 (LP-friendly), zero licensing issues. Complements owgap rather than repeating it.

2. SUPPORT2 (UCI 880) — the medical headline

n = 9,075  |  corr(X,S) = 0.47 (AUC 0.76)  |  realized Λ = 2.59  |  P(T) = 0.35  |  S→Y shift = -0.11

Hidden confounder: APS physiology score > median (hidden). Story: seriously ill hospitalized adults; the DNR order is a REAL clinical decision driven by physiologic severity (APS) that we hide; DNR patients die far more often → a naive analysis concludes DNR is lethal. The classic confounding story (same family as the famous SUPPORT right-heart-cath analyses), on public data.

Proposed design: Two experiments, mirroring the Diabetes pair but STRONGER: (B) fully real (X, S=APS, T=DNR): measured Λ=2.59 — twice Diabetes-B's 1.26 — with coupling 0.47, squarely in the PREDICTED-BOUNDARY band: the diagnostic forecasts a small/unstable O-W edge, a genuinely risky (= credible) test. (A) amplified variant on the real covariates with synthetic S/T at matched Γ=5, like Diabetes-A.

Verdict: STRONG ADD. Best realized selection strength of any candidate, publication-grade clinical narrative, n=9,075. Fills the empty middle of the diagnostic map.

3. Credit-Card Default (UCI 350) — the falsification point

n = 30,000  |  corr(X,S) = 0.03 (AUC 0.53)  |  realized Λ = 2.45  |  P(T) = 0.49  |  S→Y shift = +0.31

Hidden confounder: any past delinquency (hidden risk file). Story: the bank sets credit limits using the applicant's delinquency file (hidden to us); delinquency also drives default. Demographics alone carry almost NO information about the file: corr(X,S)=0.03.

Proposed design: Recipe B: real limit decision (Λ=2.45!) with essentially zero X–S coupling — the extreme out-of-regime point. Prediction: robust methods at matched Γ over-hedge and lose to naive; the diagnostic says DON'T USE the method here, and the experiment shows the diagnostic is right.

Verdict: ADD AS DIAGNOSTIC. Cheap (one L x Gamma sweep), and it turns 'our method can lose' into evidence FOR the paper. Pairs with Diabetes-B (0.20) to bracket the low end.

4. Adult / Census Income (UCI 2) — the recipe-A platform

n = 47,876  |  corr(X,S) = 0.27 (AUC 0.70)  |  realized Λ = — (design knob)  |  P(T) = —  |  S→Y shift = +0.29

Hidden confounder: capital-gain>0 or hours>50 (hidden drive). Story: rich, familiar socio-economic covariates (n=47,876); no natural treatment, so it contributes realism to a fully-designed experiment.

Proposed design: Recipe A at matched Γ=5 (like Diabetes-A) if a second in-regime real-X experiment is wanted; multivariate X comes for free, which would double as the multivariate robustness check the synthetic section lacks.

Verdict: OPTIONAL. Use only if reviewers demand a second ACIC-style experiment or multivariate X; otherwise redundant with Diabetes-A.

5. Student Performance (UCI 320) — natural but underpowered

n = 649  |  corr(X,S) = 0.22 (AUC 0.62)  |  realized Λ = 2.19  |  P(T) = 0.06  |  S→Y shift = +1.32

Hidden confounder: parent edu >= secondary (hidden). Story: real paid-tutoring decision (Λ=2.19 vs hidden parental education), real grades.

Proposed design: Recipe B, but n=649 and P(T)=0.06: ~39 treated students. The Wasserstein LP is fine with n but the treated arm is too thin for stable weights.

Verdict: PASS. Charming story, insufficient power.

6. Bank Marketing (UCI 222) — ruled out by measurement

n = 45,211  |  corr(X,S) = 0.14 (AUC 0.58)  |  realized Λ = 1.11  |  P(T) = 0.61  |  S→Y shift = +0.05

Hidden confounder: balance > median (hidden wealth). Story: re-contact decisions barely load on hidden wealth (Λ=1.11) and demographics do not proxy balance (corr 0.14).

Proposed design: None viable: no confounding to correct and no coupling to exploit.

Verdict: REJECT. Kept in the table as evidence the screening is real.

5. Recommended portfolio and cost

Proposal. Real-data section = Diabetes pair (done) + Mushroom (recipe C) + SUPPORT2 pair (B real + A amplified) + Credit-Default (diagnostic point). The diagnostic map then contains SIX measured couplings (0.03, 0.20, 0.47, 0.71, plus the two amplified in-regime designs at Γ=5), each with the outcome the alpha-sweep predicts — over-treatment corrected (owgap-style), under-treatment corrected (Diabetes-A-style), boundary behavior (SUPPORT2-B), and out-of-regime honesty (Diabetes-B, Credit-Default). That is the unrejectable shape: the paper ships a decision rule for practitioners, validated at every point of its range, not a collection of wins.

Compute: each new experiment is one L×Γ sweep + Kallus baseline ≈ 2–3 h on the 2-worker license (Mushroom is discrete-ish/categorical X — cheaper). Total ≈ 4 chained jobs ≈ one overnight run. All runners and the report pipeline already exist; each dataset needs one dgp.py adapter (prepare script + generate() contract), ≈ a day of work each including calibration.

7. FINAL RECOMMENDATION: the three experiments, fully specified

Combining the smoke tests (Section 3–4) with the literature conventions (Section 6): three datasets, three different recipes, three different points of the diagnostic map — together they cover baseline comparability, clinical realism, and a real-coupling win.

Experiment R1 — IST (Intl. Stroke Trial), recipe D: fully real outcomes, injected confounding

Why this one: it is the dataset of our direct baseline (Kallus & Zhou run their method on IST), outcomes need NO synthesis (both arms are real RCT arms), and a capacity cap has a natural reading (scarce anticoagulation/monitoring capacity). Reviewers of this literature expect IST; delivering it plus a head-to-head removes the easiest rejection.

Construction. T = aspirin (vs none), Y = 6-month survival/independence (real). Hidden S = baseline consciousness (RCONSC: alert vs drowsy/comatose) — strongly prognostic, clinically plausible as "unrecorded". Simulate the observational study by subsampling the RCT with P(keep | T, S) calibrated so that (i) the implied selection odds ratio is Λ = 4.95 (matched-Γ = 5 protocol identical to owgap) and (ii) P(T) ≈ 0.5. X_obs = age, sex, blood pressure, stroke subtype, deficit indicators (consciousness excluded); 1-D pipeline runs on a fitted prognostic composite of X_obs, multivariate variant optional.

Evaluation: learned policies scored on a held-out untouched RCT split by arm-matching — fully real ground truth. Regimes: uncapped + capped 30%. Smoke test PASSED (measured on the downloaded data, n = 19,285 usable): P(aspirin) = 0.500 (randomization check), S→Y shift = +0.34 (consciousness is strongly prognostic), corr(X_obs, S) = 0.57 (AUC 0.86) — the upper-boundary band of the diagnostic map, between SUPPORT2 (0.47) and Mushroom (0.71). An alternative hidden S = stroke subtype (TACS) measured corr 0.99 — REJECTED precisely because near-deterministic coupling leaves ~no Var(S|X), i.e. nothing hidden to be robust to; the check documents that the S choice is principled, not tuned.

Cost: prepare script + dgp adapter (~1 day), one chained L×Γ sweep + Kallus ≈ 2–3 h.

Experiment R2 — SUPPORT2 (UCI 880), recipe B + A: the medical headline with real selection

Why this one: the strongest REAL selection we measured (Λ = 2.59, T = DNR order, P(T) = 0.35, n = 9,075), the same SUPPORT cohort family as RHC — the canonical dataset of the MSM-Γ sensitivity literature — and a story that mirrors owgap's mechanism in a real ICU: sicker patients (hidden APS physiology) receive DNR orders and die more, so the naive analysis concludes the order itself is lethal.

Construction (two sub-experiments, mirroring the Diabetes pair but stronger). R2-B (fully real X, S, T): X_obs = age, sex, disease group, comorbidities, education, income; S = APS > median (hidden); T = real DNR order; only Y(0)/Y(1) synthesized, S-dominant and calibrated to the real death rates by (S,T) cell. Measured coupling 0.47 puts this in the PREDICTED-BOUNDARY band: the honest forecast is a small, Γ-stable O-W edge at matched Γ = 2.6 — a risky, therefore credible, test of the diagnostic. R2-A (amplified, in-regime): same real covariate bootstrap; synthetic S ~ Bern(σ(B·(x - x0))) calibrated to the real severity rate with strong coupling, synthetic T at Λ = 4.95 — the matched-Γ = 5 showcase on real clinical geometry.

Cost: prepare + adapter reuse the Diabetes pattern almost verbatim (~1 day), two sweeps ≈ 4–5 h chained.

Experiment R3 — Mushroom (UCI 73), recipe C: the real-coupling near-in-regime point

Why this one: the highest REAL coupling of any candidate (corr(X_obs, S) = 0.71, AUC 0.90) with an almost deterministic S→Y link (+0.97): odor nearly decides edibility. The classification→policy conversion is standard practice (Dudik et al.; POEM), so the construction itself is pre-approved by the literature.

Construction. A forager decided eat/pass by SMELL; the dataset we learn from records 20 morphological features but not odor. S = foul-class odor (hidden), T = ate, Y(eat) = +1 if edible / -5 if poisonous (asymmetric harm; all-eat clearly bad), Y(pass) = 0. Logging policy P(eat | X, S) uses S with strength calibrated to Λ = 4.95 given X_obs (same calibration recipe as Diabetes-A) — matched-Γ = 5 protocol intact. 1-D pipeline on the fitted edibility composite of X_obs; capped regime = basket budget 30%. Real coupling 0.71 sits just below the 0.75 in-regime line: quoted as the near-boundary real-coupling point (predicted: clear but not maximal O-W margin); the X-augmented variant (+spore-print raises coupling) is the disclosed fallback.

Cost: lightest of the three — categorical X, n = 8,124, no outcome calibration subtleties; adapter + one sweep ≈ half a day + 2 h.

What the trio buys, jointly. Recipe coverage D + B/A + C (no construction criticism applies to all three); diagnostic-map coverage at real couplings 0.47 and 0.71 plus designed Γ=5 points, with Diabetes (0.20) and optionally Credit-Default (0.03) anchoring the out-of-regime end; literature coverage — the baseline's own dataset (IST), the sensitivity literature's cohort (SUPPORT), and the bandit-conversion tradition (Mushroom). Total new compute ≈ 9–12 h of chained 2-worker solver time = one overnight run; total build effort ≈ 2–3 days.

8. Risks and honesty notes

  • Mushroom coupling sits at 0.71, just under the 0.75 in-regime line — margins may be modest; that is scientifically fine (it is the boundary point) but it is not a guaranteed blowout. The X-augmented variant is the fallback and must be disclosed as such.
  • SUPPORT2-B at coupling 0.47 is predicted-boundary: the honest expectation is a small or zero O-W edge with Γ-stability, not a win. If it loses badly, that WEAKENS the diagnostic story — this is a genuine test, which is exactly why it is credible.
  • Synthetic-outcome disclosure applies to every recipe-B experiment (Y(0)/Y(1) must be synthesized for ground truth; calibrate to real outcome rates and disclose).
  • Leakage checks needed per dataset (e.g. Diabetes 'change'/'diabetesMed' lesson: no treatment-derived columns in X_obs).
  • All smoke numbers are point estimates from one 3-fold CV logistic; rerun with the final X_obs definitions before quoting in the paper.

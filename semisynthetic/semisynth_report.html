The risk-score paper's semi-synthetic construction — run for policy learning

Their DGP on bank_marketing, extended with a heterogeneous CATE so both potential outcomes exist. Solved only at the matched Γ = e2γ, with and without their C4 "historical rationality" constraint. ntrain = 300, 10 seeds.

How the benchmark is built

Theirs, followed exactly. The UCI label is used twice — as the unobserved confounder U and as the untreated potential outcome Y0 ∈ {−1, +1}. Treatment comes from their Eq. (6), logit π0(x,u) = λ′x + γu with λj ~ U(−0.1, 0.1) and no clipping, where π0 is the probability of no treatment. The dataset passes their admissibility screen: cross-validated logistic log-loss 0.3741, inside the band [0.35, ln 2 = 0.6931].

Ours. Their construction only needs Y0, because their target is a risk score. Policy learning needs both arms, so Y1 = Y0 + τ(x) + ε with τ(x) = a (σ(b′x − c) − ½), a ~ U(0.5, 2). The −½ is load-bearing: without it τ is strictly positive (a > 0 and σ ∈ (0,1)), the CATE never changes sign, and the oracle policy is "treat everyone". Measured on this dataset, the uncentred form gives headroom of exactly +0.00000 — a benchmark on which no policy learner can be separated from a constant.

Protocol

γ ∈ {0, 1, 1.5, 2} (the paper's Table 1), each solved only at its matched Γ = e2γ ∈ {1, 7.39, 20.09, 54.60}. cε ∈ {1, 1.025, 2} — 1.025 is the paper's own ε multiplier. L ∈ {∞, 3, 1}. 300 training rows, 4,000 held-out rows with both potential outcomes retained, 10 seeds. Each (γ, seed) cell ran as its own SLURM job, 40 in parallel.

The solver's covariate is the scalar index b′x. Since τ depends on x only through b′x, that index is a sufficient statistic for the optimal policy — the projection loses nothing about which decision is correct, so this isolates confounding-robustness rather than dimension reduction. The raw d-dimensional arm with the MILP halfspace class is the natural follow-up.

Regenerate: sbatch scripts/uci10/ss_prep.sh, then scripts/uci10/ss_array.sh, then python3 aggregate_semisynth.py and python3 build_semisynth_report.py. The C4 constraint is added to the four solvers by semisynthetic/patch_rationality.py (backups in solver_backups/) and verified by test_rationality.py.

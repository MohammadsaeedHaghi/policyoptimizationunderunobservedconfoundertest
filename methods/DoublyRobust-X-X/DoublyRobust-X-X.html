DoublyRobust

Robust AIPW (doubly-robust) value maximiser — box-only. Files: Capped/doublyrobust_capped.py, Uncapped/doublyrobust_uncapped.py.

One sentence. DoublyRobust anchors the policy value on an outcome model μ̂ and makes only the leftover IPW correction robust to the unobserved confounder (the marginal-sensitivity box) — the doubly-robust analog of R-O.
Design note (new derivation). There was no doubly-robust solver in the repository. This is the chosen formulation: robust AIPW value-max, box-only. It is the R-O LP with the outcome residual in place of Y, plus the direct outcome term. Correctness is anchored by a reduction (below), not an old reference.

2 · The in-sample problem (robust AIPW value)

Write the factual residual \(r_i = Y_i - \hat\mu_{T_i}(X_i)\). DoublyRobust maximises the outcome-model value plus the worst-case residual IPW correction:

\[ \boxed{\;\max_{\pi\in\Pi}\ \underbrace{\frac1n\sum_{i}\sum_{k}\pi_k(x_i)\,\hat\mu_k(x_i)}_{\text{direct (outcome model)}}\;+\;\underbrace{\min_{w\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm O}(\Gamma)}\ \frac1n\sum_{i}\pi_{T_i}(x_i)\,w_i\,r_i}_{\text{worst-case residual correction}}\;} \]

with \(\Pi\) the valid-policy set (simplex per cell, tie on each cell, and \(\frac1n\sum_i\pi_k\le\mathrm{cap}_k\) for the capped version), and \(\mathcal U_{\mathrm O}(\Gamma)=\{w: a_i\le w_i\le b_i,\ \sum_{i:T_i=k}w_i=n\}\) — exactly R-O's box + Hájek-calibration set, here acting on the residual \(r\) instead of \(Y\).

3 · What is actually solved (dual LP)

Dualising the inner \(\min_w\) gives R-O's box-only dual with \(Y\to r\), plus the (θ-free) direct term added to the objective. Variables \(\pi_k(x_i)\); \(\mu_i,\nu_i\ge 0\); \(\beta_k\) free.

\[ \max_{\pi,\mu,\nu,\beta}\ \ \frac1n\sum_i\sum_k\pi_k(x_i)\hat\mu_k(x_i)\;+\;\sum_i\big(a_i\mu_i-b_i\nu_i\big)\;+\;\sum_k n\beta_k \]
\[ \text{s.t.}\quad \mu_i-\nu_i=\tfrac1n\,r_i\,\pi_{T_i}(x_i)-\beta_{T_i}\ \ \forall i;\qquad \textstyle\sum_k\pi_k(x_i)=1,\ \pi\ \text{tied per cell};\qquad \underbrace{\tfrac1n\textstyle\sum_i\pi_k(x_i)\le\mathrm{cap}_k}_{\textbf{capped only}};\qquad \mu_i,\nu_i\ge0. \]

\(\mu_i,\nu_i\ge0\)box lower/upper duals on \(a_i,b_i\) (acting on the residual correction)
\(\beta_k\) (free)per-arm Hájek-calibration multiplier

4 · How it is solved — R-O's dual on the residual, plus the direct term

DoublyRobust reuses R-O's dualisation verbatim on the residual correction and simply adds the (policy-only) direct outcome term back into the objective. The steps below trace how the AIPW max–min of section 2 becomes the single LP of section 3 (we write \(w\) for the adversary's weight vector and \(r_i=Y_i-\hat\mu_{T_i}(x_i)\) for the factual residual).

Step 1 — primal (max–min). Maximise the direct outcome value plus the worst-case residual correction:
\[ \max_{\pi\in\Pi}\ \left[\ \frac1n\sum_{i}\sum_{k}\pi_k(x_i)\,\hat\mu_k(x_i)\;+\;\min_{w\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm O}(\Gamma)}\ \frac1n\sum_{i}\pi_{T_i}(x_i)\,w_i\,r_i\ \right],\qquad r_i=Y_i-\hat\mu_{T_i}(x_i). \]
Step 2 — only the correction has an adversary. The direct term \(\frac1n\sum_i\sum_k\pi_k(x_i)\hat\mu_k(x_i)\) is constant in \(w\), so it passes outside the inner minimisation. The inner \(\min_w\) is therefore exactly R-O's inner problem with \(Y\) replaced by the residual \(r\):
\[ \min_{w\in\mathcal U_{\mathrm O}(\Gamma)}\ \frac1n\sum_{i}\pi_{T_i}(x_i)\,w_i\,r_i,\qquad \mathcal U_{\mathrm O}(\Gamma)=\Big\{w:\ a_i\le w_i\le b_i\ \forall i,\ \textstyle\sum_{i:T_i=k} w_i=n\ \forall k\Big\}. \]
Step 3 — dualise the inner, as in R-O. Attach box duals \(\mu_i\ge0\) (to \(w_i\ge a_i\)), \(\nu_i\ge0\) (to \(w_i\le b_i\)) and a free calibration multiplier \(\beta_k\) (to the arm-\(k\) equality). LP strong duality gives the stationarity constraint and dual objective
\[ \mu_i-\nu_i=\tfrac1n\,r_i\,\pi_{T_i}(x_i)-\beta_{T_i}\ \ \forall i,\qquad \sum_{i}\big(a_i\mu_i-b_i\nu_i\big)+\sum_{k} n\,\beta_k. \]
Step 4 — combine. Add the direct term \(\frac1n\sum_i\sum_k\pi_k(x_i)\hat\mu_k(x_i)\) (linear in \(\pi\)) to that dual objective, together with the policy constraints (simplex, per-cell tie, capacity) — yielding the single LP of section 3. Setting \(\hat\mu\equiv0\) makes \(r=Y\) and the direct term vanish, recovering R-O exactly.
Step 5 — solve. The merged LP is handed to Gurobi with DualReductions=0 so the dual certificate \((\beta,\mu,\nu)\) is well defined.

How it is used. The solve returns the on-support policy \(\pi\) (\(K\times n\)) together with the box duals; deployment to new covariates is done with the Shapley/KNN extension. The outcome-model nuisance \(\hat\mu\) comes from common.outcome_means.

5 · Correctness anchor & limits

  • μ̂ ≡ 0 ⇒ exactly R-O. With a zero outcome model the residual is \(r=Y\) and the direct term vanishes, so DoublyRobust collapses to R-O. Verified bit-identical (capped & uncapped, \(\max|\Delta\pi|=|\Delta\beta|=|\Delta\mu|=|\Delta\nu|=0\)) — and R-O is itself bit-identical to the old solver, so DoublyRobust is transitively grounded.
  • Γ = 1 ⇒ (non-robust) AIPW. The box collapses (\(w_i=\hat w_i\)) and the objective becomes the ordinary AIPW value of \(\pi\).
  • Double robustness. The estimate is consistent if either the propensity (via \(\hat w\)) or the outcome model \(\hat\mu\) is correct; the box additionally guards the propensity side against the unobserved confounder.

6 · Capped vs. Uncapped, and arguments

  • Capped adds \(\frac1n\sum_i\pi_k\le\mathrm{cap}_k\); Uncapped drops it.
  • outcome_means (n,K) — the μ̂ nuisance; use common.outcome_means(..., cross_fit=True) for an honest cross-fitted estimate.
  • discretize/mesh — discretise X to the same support as the other methods.
  • Gamma — size of the MSM box on the residual correction.

Scope: this is the box-only variant (chosen first). A box∩Wasserstein DoublyRobust-OW can be added later by mirroring R-OW's transport machinery on the residual. Outputs persisted per results/SAVING.md.
